1 - Navigate to https://jlcpcb.com in your preferred internet browser (Chrome/Safari Works well)

2 - Create Account (For Easy Recall of PCB Order Later)

3 - Click "Order now" Button on the Home Page (https://jlcpcb.com)

4 - Upload the Gerber Zip File "Gerber_Pcb1_Noise Extrapolator Platform.zip"

4 - Check the Visual Image of the Noise Extrapolator PCB Once Gerber Has Processed (around 5 to 10 mins). 
*(See Reference Images in Hardware/Reference Images_JLCPCB.com-Ordering PCBs Folder

5 - Use Default Settings Order Required amount of PCBs (No need to change any unless customisation of material is desired).

*Take note of the price increase past 10 or more PCB units on the order, as international shipping rates can vastly increase with weight. 15 is a good compromise for a first run.
